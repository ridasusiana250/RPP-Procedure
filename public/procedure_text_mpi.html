🌟 Communicative Purpose (Social Function)

The primary purpose of a Procedure Text is to describe how something is accomplished or completed through a sequence of actions or steps.

Key Contexts in Daily Life:
  • Helping users operate electronic gadgets safely without damage.
  • Guiding cooks to produce consistent culinary flavor and food safety.
  • Preventing accidents during laboratory experiments or emergency drills.
  • Standardizing professional workflows (e.g., flight checklists, medical CPR).

💡 Deep Learning Insight: A procedure text is not just words—it is a safety shield and an empowering roadmap for human action.

🎯 Target Audience & Tone

A high-quality procedure text must be calibrated to its audience's prior knowledge and emotional state:

Clear & Concise (Direct Tone): Avoid poetic metaphors or storytelling. Users need fast, unambiguous instructions.
Chronological Logic: Steps must follow temporal order. Step 3 cannot be executed before Step 1 without catastrophic failure.
Action-Oriented: Employs clear commands starting with bare infinitives (imperative verbs).

How to Brew Traditional Indonesian Herbal Ginger Drink (Wedang Jahe)
Ingredients & Utensils:
  • 100 grams of fresh ginger root (crushed/bruised)
  • 40 grams of organic palm sugar (gula aren)
  • 1 stalk of lemongrass (crushed) and 1 cinnamon stick
  • 500 ml of filtered water
Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. First, wash the ginger thoroughly and roast it briefly over an open flame to release its aromatic oils.
  2. Next, crush the roasted ginger and lemongrass with a pestle.
  3. Then, pour the 500 ml of water into a small pot and bring it to a gentle boil.
  4. After that, add the crushed ginger, lemongrass, cinnamon, and palm sugar into the boiling water. Simmer for 10 minutes over low heat.
  5. Finally, strain the warm herbal mixture into a glass and serve immediately.

🔤 1. Imperative Sentences & Action Verbs

Procedure texts give instructions using commands. They omit the subject "You" and begin straight with a bare infinitive (V1).

❌ You should peel the potatoes carefully. (Too descriptive/modal)

✅ Peel the potatoes carefully. (Direct Imperative Command)

❌ Don't to touch the hot wire.

✅ Do not touch the exposed wire. (Negative Imperative)

⏳ 2. Connectives & Temporal Conjunctions

Sequencing adverbs maintain chronological order so the reader never gets confused about what happens when.

Start: First, Initially, To begin with
Middle: Next, Then, After that, Subsequently
Simultaneous: Meanwhile, While, At the same time
End: Finally, Lastly, In the end

📍 3. Adverbial Phrases of Manner, Time, & Place

Provide specific detail on HOW, FOR HOW LONG, or WHERE to execute an action.

  • Manner: Whisk the eggs vigorously.
  • Time: Bake the dough for 25 minutes at 180°C.
  • Place: Insert the SIM card into tray slot 1.

⚡ 4. Simple Present Tense & Action Verbs

Always written in general factual present tense. Focuses on generalized human agents rather than a specific narrator's past experience.

Key Action Verbs: Mix, tighten, slice, reboot, adjust, configure, dilute, secure, verify.

🍲 1. Culinary Recipes (Resep)

Details how to prepare edible dishes or drinks. Focuses on ingredients, exact measurements (grams, teaspoons), cooking temperature, and duration.

Example: How to Cook Authentic Rendang Daging Sapi

📱 2. User Manuals (Buku Petunjuk Alat)

Explains how to operate, assemble, or maintain mechanical, electronic, or digital devices safely. Heavy focus on parts and safety warnings.

Example: How to Pair Bluetooth Earbuds with a Laptop

💡 3. Tips & Life Hacks (Kiat Sukses)

Offers practical behavioral advice or strategies to improve habits, productivity, study routines, or personal safety.

Example: 5 Smart Tips to Protect Your Social Media from Hackers

🛡️ 4. Protocols & Safety Rules (SOP / Regulasi)

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) or mandatory public conduct guides designed to maintain order, health, and disaster preparedness.

Example: Safety Protocol: What to Do During an Earthquake in a High-Rise School
